# Audience research and channel plan — 15 November 2026

## Evidence

- Gallup, global emotional wellbeing, 2025 fieldwork: 38% of adults reported substantial worry and 36% substantial stress the previous day. These figures concern daily experience, not diagnoses or the channel audience. https://www.gallup.com/405494/indicator-global-emotional-wellbeing.aspx
- WHO Commission on Social Connection, 2025: approximately one in six people globally experience loneliness. https://www.who.int/news-room/questions-and-answers/item/social-connection
- CBOS, financial assessments, 2026: financial wellbeing has improved, while anxiety about future finances remains higher than before the pandemic. Do not equate current hardship with every household. https://www.cbos.pl/SPISKOM.POL/2026/K_044_26.PDF
- CBOS, consumer mood, October 2026: concern increased relative to August for the economy, the future and household finances. https://cbos.pl/PL/publikacje/flashe_tekst.php?nr=36%2F2026

These sources support priorities around money insecurity, mental pressure and social connection. Digital availability, boundaries, envy, emotional masking and opinion defensiveness are creative hypotheses derived from those tensions; this is not a measured top-ten prevalence ranking.
